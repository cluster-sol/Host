FAQ

Common Estimation & Billing Questions

How does the 40/30/30 milestone payment schedule work?

You only pay 40% upfront to initiate discovery, Figma UI/UX wireframes, database schemas, and GitHub scaffolding. Once the staging build is deployed and passes User Acceptance Testing (UAT), you pay 30%. The final 30% is only paid upon live production cutover and full IP source code handover.

Do you charge extra for international payments or foreign currency?

No. International clients (US, UK, EU, UAE, Singapore) are billed under our approved GST Letter of Undertaking (LUT ARN: AD070426008921L) at 0% IGST (Zero-Rated Software Export). We support flat-rate Stripe USD billing or direct SWIFT/Wire transfer with automatic e-FIRA generation.

Who owns the code, intellectual property, and database?

You own 100% of the intellectual property. Upon final invoice clearance, the private GitHub repository, Figma files, cloud credentials, and vector database embeddings are completely transferred to your organization. We sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to project kickoff.
