## The 3-in-1 Agency Architecture

An enterprise-grade IT & AI agency website should not be a static brochure. We operate as a **unified platform**:

- **The Public Conversion Engine:** Featuring interactive AI architectural scoping terminals, real-time node visualizers, and instant project cost & ROI calculators.
- **The Authenticated Client Portal (`/portal`):** Where clients log in to inspect live Kanban sprint velocity, launch staging builds, test Android APKs, and review live AI telemetry.
- **The Stamped Tax Invoice Studio (`/invoice`):** Complete with pure vector XML logos, SAC codes, and official circular rubber seals compliant with Indian GST and international LUT export guidelines.
