This Privacy Policy describes how {{ site.company.legal_name }} ("Cluster Sol", "Company", "we", "our", or "us") collects, processes, and protects your personal data when you visit our website ({{ site.url }}), use our interactive project estimators, or engage our custom software and AI development services.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to scope, propose, and deliver enterprise technology contracts:

  • Client Inbound Data: Name, work email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, company name, project requirements, and budget ranges submitted via our contact and estimator forms.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser headers, IP country codes, and user-agent strings processed by Cloudflare edge networks for security and rate-limiting purposes.
  • AI & Project Data: Information, documents, and technical schemas shared during technical audits and discovery sessions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Review technical feasibility and draft official Scopes of Work (SOWs).
  • Sync inbound leads to our internal Google Sheets intake ledger and founder alert channels.
  • Generate tax invoices, manage 40/30/30 milestone billing, and comply with Goods and Services Tax (GST) reporting.
  • Prevent automated bot spam, credential stuffing, and edge DDoS attacks.

3. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to data brokers or advertising networks. We only share data with infrastructure subprocessors required to fulfill our contracts (e.g., Cloudflare, Google Cloud / Apps Script, Supabase, Stripe, and Razorpay).

4. Data Security & Retention

All web traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. Production credentials, API tokens, and client secrets are stored exclusively in dedicated encrypted vaults. Client data is retained for the duration of the engagement plus statutory tax audit retention periods (7 years under Indian tax regulations).

5. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

You possess the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise your data rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at {{ site.company.email }}.
