2. 40/30/30 Milestone Payment Governance

Unless mutually agreed otherwise in an executed SOW, all sprint contracts follow our standard milestone framework:

  • 40% Kickoff Advance: Non-refundable retainer due prior to commencement of architecture, Figma wireframes, and repository scaffolding.
  • 30% Staging UAT Sign-off: Due upon deployment of the working staging build and completion of client acceptance testing.
