This AI Ethics & LLM Hallucination Disclaimer outlines the operational parameters, risk mitigations, and inherent boundaries of Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engines, and autonomous agent workflows developed by {{ site.company.legal_name }}.

1. Probabilistic Nature of Generative AI

Clients acknowledge that LLMs (including models provided by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and open-source foundations) are probabilistic language prediction systems. While Cluster Sol enforces state-of-the-art semantic guardrails, strict temperature controls, and vector citation constraints, generative AI models can occasionally produce inaccurate, misleading, or unexpected assertions ("hallucinations").

2. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop for Critical Operations

We strongly advise against deploying fully autonomous AI models for high-liability automated actions—including medical diagnosis, definitive legal counsel, autonomous stock trading, or irrevocable credit card billing—without human-in-the-loop review.

3. Third-Party Foundation Model Outages & Drift

Cluster Sol is not responsible for upstream third-party foundation provider service outages, model deprecations, latency spikes, or policy modifications initiated by API vendors (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Twilio).

4. Client Data Privacy & Zero Training Policy

All enterprise AI pipelines engineered by Cluster Sol utilize commercial enterprise API tiers with zero-data retention guarantees (i.e., your confidential PDFs, customer tickets, and database records are NOT used by upstream LLM providers to train public models).
