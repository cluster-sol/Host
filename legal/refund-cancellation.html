Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Because {{ site.company.legal_name }} provides custom, high-touch engineering, human intellectual capital, and architecture labor, our refund and cancellation policies reflect our milestone delivery structure.

1. Advance Milestone Retainers

The 40% Kickoff Advance Payment covers preliminary system architecture design, database entity modeling, Figma user journey mockups, and resource provisioning. Once sprint work has commenced, the 40% Kickoff Advance is strictly non-refundable.

2. Project Cancellation Procedure

Either party may terminate an ongoing engagement by providing 14 calendar days written notice via email to {{ site.company.billing_email }}.

  • If cancellation is initiated by the Client prior to Staging UAT, the Client will be billed on a pro-rata basis for verified sprint hours expended up to the date of cancellation.
  • All custom code and design assets completed and paid for up to the termination date will be packaged and delivered to the Client.

3. Completed Milestones

Once a milestone deliverable has been demonstrated, verified on the staging environment, and approved via client sign-off (e.g., UAT sign-off), payments associated with that milestone are deemed fully earned and non-refundable.

4. Monthly Retainers (AMC)

Monthly DevOps & AI Retainer contracts may be cancelled by providing 15 days notice prior to the start of the subsequent monthly billing cycle. Unused hours within a billed monthly period do not roll over and are not eligible for cash refunds.

5. Dispute Resolution Window

If you believe an invoice was processed in error or an unauthorized transaction occurred, please notify our accounts desk at {{ site.company.billing_email }} within 7 business days of invoice generation for prompt investigation.
